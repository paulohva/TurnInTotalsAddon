# Changelog

All notable changes to Turn-in Totals are listed here.

## Unreleased

## 1.0.0

- First release: at the top of the quest log, the number of quests ready to turn in and the total XP
  and gold they give.
- Shown only when at least one quest is ready. At the maximum level only the gold shows.
- Option to turn it off (Options > AddOns > Turn-in Totals). Off, the quest log is left exactly as
  the game made it.
- `/tit` prints the totals in the chat.
- English and Português (Brasil).
