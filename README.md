# Turn-in Totals

A small addon for **WoW: Forever**: at the top of the quest log, how many quests are ready to turn
in and how much **XP** and **gold** they give together.

```
Ready to turn in (6 quests)
8,935 XP   1g 23s 45c
```

- Hover over the line for the list of those quests, each with its own XP and gold.
- Counts every quest in your log that is ready to turn in, whatever the search box shows.
- Shows nothing when no quest is ready. At the maximum level only the gold shows.
- Doesn't touch the quest list itself: the line sits above the search box, and the list moves down
  to make room.

## Options

Options > AddOns > Turn-in Totals: **Show in the quest log** (on by default). Turned off, the line
goes away at once; after a `/reload` with it off, the addon doesn't touch the quest log at all.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tit` | Print the totals in the chat |

## Install

Extract the `TurnInTotals` folder into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`.

## Languages

English and Português (Brasil).
